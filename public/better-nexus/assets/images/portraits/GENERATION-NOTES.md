# Hero portrait assets
Five fictional remote-professional portraits created with the built-in Image Generation tool for this project. Illustrative talent imagery; these are not employee photographs.

Prompt set: photorealistic editorial, one centered person at a laptop in a contemporary workspace, natural skin, soft daylight, subtle teal accents, no logos/text/watermarks. Subjects: Indian female specialist in teal; Black male developer in navy; East Asian creative director with glasses; South Asian male finance specialist in ivory; Latina support professional with headset.

Final files: portrait-01.webp through portrait-05.webp. Converted to optimized WebP for website use.
